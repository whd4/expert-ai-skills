# Why Monte Carlo?

OpenClaw's future depends on **many uncertain variables**:

- Will security issues be resolved?
- Will the hosted platform monetize?
- Will AI providers block access?
- Will the founder sustain momentum?
- Will regulators intervene?

**Monte Carlo simulation** models compound uncertainty by running
**10,000 random simulations** for each scenario, producing
**probability distributions** instead of single-point guesses.

# Methodology Notes

- **10,000 Monte Carlo simulations** per scenario per horizon
- **Day-by-day simulation** of growth, churn, revenue, incidents
- **Poisson process** for security incidents
- **Gaussian noise** on growth rates with scenario-specific parameters
- **Anchored to real data** as of January 31, 2026
